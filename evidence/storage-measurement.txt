Portable Local Studio - disk measurement of the simulated 128 GB USB drive
==========================================================================

Test PC     : HP Victus laptop, Intel i5-12450H, 31.7 GB RAM, NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop 4 GB VRAM, Windows 11 Home 10.0.26300
Drive       : "subst U: C:\AI\usb128" makes a folder look like drive U:. The app ran only from U:\Portable-Local-Studio.
Limit       : audit only. NTFS puts no 128 GB cap on this folder. Real USB speed and exFAT were not tested.
Method      : PowerShell, Get-ChildItem -Recurse -File -Force, sum of file Length (bytes). 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes.

1. Totals
---------
Baseline, app + 2 chat models only (2026-11-01T07:06:09)
  U:\ files=10998  bytes=5,720,534,998   (5.72 GB)

After all tests: chat, image, speech, voice and their outputs (measured 2026-11-01T08:48:09)
  U:\ files=11021  bytes=8,096,075,478   (8.10 GB = 7.54 GiB)

This is the largest total measured. A 128 GB drive holds about 119.2 GiB before formatting overhead,
so the studio used about 6.3 % of it, and about 111.7 GiB stayed free.

6. Check for files written outside the drive
--------------------------------------------
Start marker: 2026-11-01T07:09:15 (before the image, speech and voice tests).
After the tests, we listed every file larger than 100 KB that changed after the marker in:
  %USERPROFILE%\.cache
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\npm-cache
  %APPDATA%\NVIDIA\ComputeCache
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\NVIDIA\DXCache and GLCache
  %TEMP%
  %USERPROFILE%\Downloads
Result:
  - No studio model, cache or output was outside U:.
  - The Hugging Face cache, the npm cache and the NVIDIA ComputeCache did not change.
  - The only writes were the test browser's own profile and the NVIDIA DXCache (browser GPU shader cache).
Two ways to put files on C: anyway:
  - The first setup uses %LOCALAPPDATA%\npm-cache unless you run "set npm_config_cache=%CD%\app\tools\npm-cache" first.
  - The Download / TXT / JSON / Download WAV buttons in the browser save a copy to your Downloads folder on C:.
